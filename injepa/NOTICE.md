# Source attribution

The InJepa implementation builds on [INTACT-JEPA](https://github.com/zju3dv/INTACT-JEPA), developed by Junhan Sun, Hao Zhao and Guofeng Zhang, at revision `235b6a3a92db4d0f1b3a40597ab1f407db4fd15b`. Its MIT copyright notice is preserved in `LICENSE`.

INTACT incorporates modules derived from [LeWM](https://github.com/lucas-maes/le-wm), developed by Lucas Maes, Quentin Le Lidec, Damien Scieur, Yann LeCun and Randall Balestriero. The upstream MIT notice is preserved in `third_party/LeWM-MIT-LICENSE.txt`.

The source adds the InJepa goal-conditioned proposal, recurrent candidate construction, training objectives and navigation evaluation adapters. Component revisions and usage are indexed in the repository's `THIRD_PARTY.md` and `README.md`.
